Explore Nature

Mist-Covered Mountains and Emerald Valleys

Immerse yourself in the breathtaking natural beauty of Sri Lanka. From the rolling emerald hills of the tea country to the majestic waterfalls plunging down the central highlands, the island's interior offers a haven for nature lovers.

Places to Experience

Horton Plains National Park

A protected national park in the central highlands featuring montane grassland and cloud forest. The highlight is the hike to 'World's End', a sheer precipice with a 870-meter drop offering stunning views.

Sinharaja Forest Reserve

A UNESCO World Heritage site and the last viable area of primary tropical rainforest in Sri Lanka. It is a treasure trove of endemic species, rare birds, and incredible biodiversity.

Knuckles Mountain Range

Named for its resemblance to a clenched fist, this rugged mountain range is perfect for trekking. It features isolated villages, cascading waterfalls, and misty peaks untouched by mass tourism.

Bambarakanda Falls

The tallest waterfall in Sri Lanka, cascading down a sheer rock face in a dramatic drop of 263 meters. Surrounded by pine forests, it offers a breathtaking hiking experience.

Riverston Peak

Located in the Knuckles Mountain Range, Riverston offers some of the most spectacular, windy, and misty viewpoints in the country, known affectionately as the 'Mini World's End'.

Diyaluma Falls

Sri Lanka's second-highest waterfall. Hike to the upper falls to discover a series of natural rock pools perfect for a refreshing swim right at the edge of the cliff.
