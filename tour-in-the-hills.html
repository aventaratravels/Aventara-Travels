Tour in the Hills

Explore the scenic hill country and experience its heritage and natural beauty.

Itinerary Overview

Day 01 - Airport | Negombo +

Airport | Negombo

Meet and assist on arrival at Airport by Aventara Travels Representative. Transfer from Airport to Negombo. Just seven kilometres from the Bandaranaike International Airport, at the tip of the Negombo lagoon, lays the pristine Negombo beach. Negombo is a famous fishing village that is proud of its colonial Dutch fort and other Dutch period architecture.

Savour a delectable dinner at a hotel in Negombo before retiring for the night to rest and recover from your flight.

Day 02 - Kandy | Cultural Heritage +

Kandy | Cultural Heritage

Surrounded by mountains and lush tea estates, Kandy is Sri Lanka's cultural capital and the last stronghold of the ancient Sinhalese kings. This UNESCO World Heritage site is famous worldwide for the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), one of the most venerable Buddhist temples in the world.

Visitors can enjoy leisurely walks around the picturesque Kandy Lake, explore the incredibly diverse Royal Botanical Gardens of Peradeniya, and witness the captivating rhythms of a traditional Kandyan cultural dance performance. Kandy's cool climate and rich heritage make it an unforgettable part of any itinerary.

Day 03 - Nuwara Eliya | Tea Country +

Nuwara Eliya | Tea Country

Journey into the cool, refreshing mountain air and picturesque vistas of Nuwara Eliya, famously known as 'Little England'. En-route, feast your eyes on cascading waterfalls and emerald slopes carpeted with lush tea bushes.

Visit a sprawling tea estate and factory, where you will learn how the finest Ceylon tea in the world is plucked, processed, and packaged. Finish the tour with a freshly brewed cup of fragrant tea. In Nuwara Eliya, take a stroll along the banks of Lake Gregory or indulge in freshly picked strawberries at a local farm before enjoying a cozy evening in the highlands.

Day 04 - Ella | Scenic Highlands +

Ella | Scenic Highlands

Experience the magic of the Sri Lankan highlands. Depending on your routing, you may experience one of the world's most scenic train rides winding through mountain valleys and dense pine forests.

Arrive in the laid-back town of Ella, a paradise for nature lovers. Hike up Little Adam's Peak for breathtaking panoramic views of the Ella Gap, and visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge, an architectural masterpiece hidden amidst the lush jungle and tea estates. Relax in the cool mountain climate and enjoy a peaceful evening.

Day 05 - Departure +

Departure

Enjoy a hearty breakfast at your hotel and spend the morning at your leisure, perhaps picking up some last-minute souvenirs or taking one final stroll along the beach.

Proceed to the Bandaranaike International Airport in time for your departure flight. Aventara Travels wishes you a safe flight back home, carrying unforgettable memories of your tropical island getaway.
