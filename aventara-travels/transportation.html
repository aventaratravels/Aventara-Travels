Transportation

Travel Sri Lanka Your Way

At Aventara Travels, the journey is just as important as the destination. We provide comfortable, reliable, and flexible transportation solutions designed around the needs of every traveller.

Every journey we arrange is designed with your comfort and convenience in mind. We work with trusted transportation partners and carefully coordinate vehicles, chauffeurs, guides, and transfers according to your itinerary. From the moment you arrive in Sri Lanka until your departure, Aventara Travels is here to make every kilometre of your journey effortless.

Private Cars & SUVs

Travel in comfort and privacy with a dedicated vehicle and professional chauffeur. Our private cars and SUVs are ideal for couples, families, and individual travellers looking for a relaxed and flexible journey.

Vans & Mini Coaches

For families, friends, and small groups, our spacious vans and mini coaches provide a comfortable way to explore Sri Lanka together while enjoying the freedom to travel at your own pace.

Coaches

Travelling with a larger group? We can arrange comfortable coaches suitable for group tours, corporate travel, events, and special-interest journeys.

Chauffeur-Driven Journeys

Enjoy Sri Lanka without worrying about navigation, traffic, or unfamiliar roads. Our experienced chauffeur partners know the island's destinations and routes, allowing you to sit back, relax, and enjoy the scenery. Where required, we can also arrange professional English-speaking national guides and language-speaking guides.

Scenic Train Journeys

Experience one of Sri Lanka's most memorable ways to travel. We can assist with arranging train journeys through the island's spectacular landscapes, including the famous hill-country routes surrounded by tea plantations, mountains, forests, and valleys.

Domestic Air & Helicopter Transfers

For a truly exclusive experience, Aventara can assist with arranging helicopter and private aviation services where available. Enjoy spectacular aerial views of Sri Lanka while reducing travel time between selected destinations. Ideal for luxury travellers, special occasions, and VIP journeys.
