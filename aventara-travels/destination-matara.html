Matara

Southern coastline, historic forts and beautiful beaches.

Discover Matara

Matara is a bustling commercial hub on the southern coast of Sri Lanka that beautifully blends modern development with a rich colonial history. The Nilwala River flows through the city, adding to its scenic coastal charm. The city is renowned for its pristine beaches, particularly Polhena Beach, which is famous for its calm waters and coral reefs.

History enthusiasts will appreciate the Star Fort, a unique star-shaped fortress built by the Dutch to protect the main Matara Fort. Visitors can also explore the picturesque Pigeon Island, accessible by a suspension bridge, which houses a serene Buddhist temple. Matara offers a perfect mix of cultural heritage and tropical relaxation.
