Nature and Adventure

The Ultimate Eco & Adventure Experience

From the arid jungles of Udawalawe to the gushing white-water rapids of Kithulgala, our eco package tours are carefully designed to offer adventurers a true sense of nature travel, making us an ideal travel partner in Sri Lanka for ardent explorers.

Aventara Travels will let you pick and choose from a wide range of adventurous things to do in Sri Lanka, catering to your every want! Whether you seek the thrill of the wild or the adrenaline rush of outdoor sports, we have the perfect adventure waiting for you.

White-Water Rafting in Kithulgala

Experience the rush of navigating the thrilling rapids of the Kelani River in Kithulgala. This adrenaline-pumping activity is perfect for adventure seekers of all skill levels, surrounded by dense, lush rainforests.

Udawalawe Jungle Safari

Embark on a rugged jeep safari through the arid landscapes of Udawalawe National Park. Get incredibly close to massive herds of wild Asian elephants in one of the most reliable elephant viewing spots in the world.

Hiking the Knuckles Range

For serious trekkers, the Knuckles Mountain Range offers off-the-beaten-path trails through misty cloud forests, cascading waterfalls, and isolated villages, providing a profound connection to untouched nature.

Surfing in Arugam Bay

Catch world-class waves at Arugam Bay, a surfer's paradise located on the vibrant east coast. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro, the breaks here are legendary.

Canyoning and Waterfall Abseiling

Test your limits by canyoning down natural rock slides and abseiling down spectacular waterfalls. This is the ultimate test of courage and an unforgettable way to experience Sri Lanka's cascading waters.
