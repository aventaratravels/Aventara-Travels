Luxury Sri Lanka

12 Days / 11 Nights - The Ultimate Premium Experience

Experience Sri Lanka in unparalleled luxury. This exclusive 12-day journey combines the island's most iconic destinations with stays in award-winning boutique hotels, private guided experiences, fine dining, and ultimate relaxation.

Itinerary Outline

DAY 01 - Airport | Kotugoda +

Arrive in style and relax in a luxurious boutique manor just outside the city.

DAY 02 - Kotugoda | Cultural Triangle +

Take a scenic drive to a luxury eco-resort nestled in the Cultural Triangle.

DAY 03 - Sigiriya & Private Safari +

Climb Sigiriya with a private guide, followed by an exclusive evening safari with sundowners.

DAY 04 - Cultural Triangle | Kandy +

Transfer to a heritage luxury hotel in Kandy and experience a private tour of the Temple of the Tooth.

DAY 05 - Kandy | Tea Country +

Travel into the highlands and check into a restored colonial tea planter's bungalow.

DAY 06 - Tea Country Experiences +

Enjoy a private tea tasting session, gourmet dining, and a guided nature walk through the estates.

DAY 07 - Tea Country | Yala +

Fly or drive to the deep south and arrive at a luxury tented camp bordering Yala National Park.

DAY 08 - Yala Wilderness +

Experience a premium private safari with expert naturalists, complete with a bush dinner under the stars.

DAY 09 - Yala | Weligama/Galle +

Travel to the exclusive southern coast and check into a spectacular cliffside luxury resort.

DAY 10 - Galle Fort & Coast +

Enjoy a privately guided walk through the historic Galle Fort and dine at an award-winning restaurant.

DAY 11 - Southern Coast +

A day of complete relaxation. Indulge in luxury spa treatments or a private yacht cruise.

DAY 12 - Coast | Airport +

Conclude your luxurious journey with a private transfer back to the airport.

What's Included

  • Airport meet & assist
  • Private air-conditioned vehicle with English-speaking chauffeur/guide
  • Premium accommodation with daily breakfast
  • Sightseeing and transportation as mentioned
  • 24/7 Local assistance from Aventara Travels
