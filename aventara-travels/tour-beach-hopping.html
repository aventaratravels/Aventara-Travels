8 Days / 7 Nights

Beach Hopping Adventures

Welcome to a world of soft sands and crystalline waters for a true tropical escape.

Itinerary Overview

Day 01 - Airport | Negombo +

Airport | Negombo

Meet and assist on arrival at Airport by Aventara Travels Representative. Transfer from Airport to Negombo. Just seven kilometres from the Bandaranaike International Airport, at the tip of the Negombo lagoon, lays the pristine Negombo beach. Negombo is a famous fishing village that is proud of its colonial Dutch fort and other Dutch period architecture.

Savour a delectable dinner at a hotel in Negombo before retiring for the night to rest and recover from your flight.

Day 02 - Trincomalee | East Coast Paradise +

Trincomalee | East Coast Paradise

Arrive in Trincomalee, a destination celebrated for its natural deep-water harbour, beautiful white-sand beaches, and turquoise waters. Spend the day relaxing and enjoying the tropical coastal atmosphere of the east coast.

You may visit the historic Koneswaram Temple, a classical-medieval Hindu temple complex perched high on a cliff overlooking the ocean. For the adventurous, embark on an optional excursion to Pigeon Island for world-class snorkelling amongst vibrant coral reefs, or set out by boat in search of playful pods of dolphins.

Day 04 - Southern Beach | Coastal Relaxation +

Southern Beach | Coastal Relaxation

Escape to a secluded heaven on the golden shores of Sri Lanka's south coast. Pristine beaches framed by swaying palm trees and kissed by warm azure blue ocean await you. Spend your day at absolute leisure, basking under the warm tropical sun or taking a dip in the ocean.

For those seeking adventure, the area is famous for exhilarating water sports. Alternatively, enjoy a serene boat ride down the tranquil Madu River, taking in the thriving wildlife inhabiting the shady mangroves and visiting tiny islands. Savour a delectable dinner by the coast.

Day 06 - Galle | Southern Heritage +

Galle | Southern Heritage

Travel along the stunning southern coastline to the historic city of Galle. Wander around the UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Galle Dutch Fort, which was originally built by the Portuguese in 1588 and extensively fortified by the Dutch.

Walk along its cobbled streets lined with carefully preserved colonial buildings, shop at chic little concept stores, and visit the Dutch Church and Museum to learn about the fortification's rich history. Enjoy the stunning views of the Indian Ocean from the iconic lighthouse before retiring for the evening.

Day 07 - Colombo City Tour +

Colombo City Tour

Proceed to the city of Colombo, the vibrant commercial capital of Sri Lanka. Witness the amazing beauty of this place that fosters a richly diverse population and a seamless blend of the old and the new.

Take a tour of the city and visit iconic landmarks including the Old Parliament, Independence Memorial Hall, National Museum, and the beautiful Gangaramaya Temple. Spend some time shopping at local boutiques or the Pettah Bazaar before enjoying a farewell dinner at a local restaurant renowned for its authentic Sri Lankan cuisine.

Day 08 - Departure +

Departure

Enjoy a hearty breakfast at your hotel and spend the morning at your leisure, perhaps picking up some last-minute souvenirs or taking one final stroll along the beach.

Proceed to the Bandaranaike International Airport in time for your departure flight. Aventara Travels wishes you a safe flight back home, carrying unforgettable memories of your tropical island getaway.
