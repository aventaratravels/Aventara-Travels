Wildlife & Adventure

The Ultimate Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable adventure into the wild. Sri Lanka boasts one of the highest rates of biological endemism in the world, with spectacular national parks offering incredible safari experiences.

Places to Experience

Minneriya National Park

Famous for 'The Gathering' during the dry season, where hundreds of wild Asian elephants congregate around the ancient Minneriya tank to graze and bathe.

Udawalawe National Park

With its open grasslands and large reservoir, Udawalawe guarantees elephant sightings year-round. It is also home to water buffalo, crocodiles, and diverse birdlife.

Wilpattu National Park

Sri Lanka's largest and oldest national park, characterized by natural, sand-rimmed water basins called 'Villus'. It is a prime location to spot leopards and sloth bears in a dense jungle setting.

Kumana National Park

Renowned for its incredible avifauna. Kumana is a paradise for bird watchers, hosting vast flocks of migratory waterfowl and wading birds in its extensive mangrove swamps.

Bundala National Park

An internationally important wintering ground for migratory water birds in Sri Lanka. It is a Ramsar wetland and a UNESCO biosphere reserve, offering quiet, pristine wildlife encounters.

Pigeon Island Snorkeling

Explore the vibrant marine life and stunning coral reefs of Pigeon Island.
