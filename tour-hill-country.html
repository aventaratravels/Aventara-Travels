Hill Country Escape

5 Days / 4 Nights - Mist, Tea, and Waterfalls

Escape to the cool, refreshing highlands of Sri Lanka. This short but unforgettable journey takes you through rolling tea estates, cascading waterfalls, and misty mountain peaks, offering a serene and rejuvenating retreat.

Itinerary Outline

DAY 01 - Airport | Kandy +

Arrive and drive directly into the hills to the cultural capital of Kandy.

DAY 02 - Kandy | Nuwara Eliya +

Travel past spectacular waterfalls into the 'Little England' of Nuwara Eliya.

DAY 03 - Nuwara Eliya | Horton Plains +

Take a morning trek through Horton Plains National Park to World's End, followed by a tea tasting experience.

DAY 04 - Nuwara Eliya | Ella +

Board the scenic train to Ella and visit the iconic Nine Arch Bridge.

DAY 05 - Ella | Airport +

Enjoy a morning hike to Little Adam's Peak before making the scenic descent back to the airport.

  • Private air-conditioned vehicle with English-speaking chauffeur/guide
  • Premium accommodation with daily breakfast
  • Sightseeing and transportation as mentioned
