Tailor-Made Sri Lanka Holidays for UK Travellers

Aventara Travels is a Sri Lankan travel agency and destination management company creating personalised journeys for travellers visiting Sri Lanka from the United Kingdom.

Whether you are planning your first visit to Sri Lanka, a romantic honeymoon, a family holiday, a wildlife adventure or a relaxing beach escape, we can design an itinerary around your interests, preferred accommodation and travel dates.

From the cultural heritage of Kandy and Sigiriya to the tea-covered mountains of Nuwara Eliya and Ella, wildlife experiences in national parks and the beaches of the southern and eastern coast, Sri Lanka offers an extraordinary variety of experiences within one island.

Our local team coordinates accommodation, private transportation, sightseeing, excursions, guides and other destination services to help make your Sri Lankan holiday comfortable and memorable.

Popular Places to Visit

Colombo

Discover Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital, combining modern city life, food, shopping and history.

Kandy

Explore cultural heritage, temples, traditions and the scenic gateway to Sri Lanka's central highlands.

Sigiriya

Visit the iconic Lion Rock and discover ancient history surrounded by spectacular landscapes.

Nuwara Eliya

Experience tea plantations, cool mountain scenery and colonial charm in Sri Lanka's hill country.

Ella

Enjoy mountain scenery, hiking, waterfalls and one of the world's most scenic railway journeys.

Yala

Experience exciting wildlife safaris and explore one of Sri Lanka's most famous national parks.

Galle

Discover the historic Galle Fort, coastal architecture, boutique experiences and southern Sri Lankan culture.

Mirissa

Enjoy tropical beaches, ocean experiences and relaxed coastal holidays.
