YOUR SRI LANKAN ADVENTURE

Why Visit Sri Lanka from Poland?

Sri Lanka offers a remarkable combination of tropical beaches, cultural heritage, wildlife, mountains, tea plantations and authentic local experiences. A single holiday can combine many different landscapes and experiences.

Tropical Beaches

Relax on beautiful beaches along Sri Lanka's southern, western and eastern coastline. Enjoy peaceful resorts, ocean views and unforgettable sunsets.

Ancient Heritage

Discover historic temples, ancient kingdoms, UNESCO World Heritage Sites and the fascinating cultural history of Sri Lanka.

Wildlife & Safari

Experience Sri Lanka's national parks and look for elephants, leopards, monkeys, birds and many other species on professionally arranged safari experiences.

Tea Country

Travel into the cool central highlands and discover tea plantations, mountain scenery, waterfalls and beautiful hill-country landscapes.

Scenic Train Journeys

Experience one of Sri Lanka's most memorable journeys through the mountains, tea plantations and valleys between Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella.

Local Experiences

Meet local communities, discover Sri Lankan cuisine, experience traditional culture and enjoy authentic experiences beyond ordinary sightseeing.

EXPLORE SRI LANKA

Popular Places to Visit

Colombo

Explore Sri Lanka's lively commercial capital, restaurants, shopping, history and coastal atmosphere.

Kandy

Discover Sri Lankan culture, the Temple of the Tooth and beautiful mountain surroundings.

Sigiriya

Visit the iconic Lion Rock and explore the surrounding cultural and natural attractions.

Nuwara Eliya

Experience cool mountain weather, tea plantations, waterfalls and colonial-era charm.

Ella

Enjoy mountain views, hiking trails, waterfalls and Sri Lanka's famous scenic railway.

Galle

Explore the historic Galle Fort and the beautiful southern coastline.
