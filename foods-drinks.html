Foods & Drinks

A Journey of Flavours

Sri Lankan cuisine is a vibrant celebration of flavour, colour, and tradition. Influenced by the island's diverse communities, tropical climate, and centuries of cultural exchange, Sri Lankan food combines aromatic spices, fresh local ingredients, coconut, herbs, and bold flavours.

From a traditional home-style rice and curry to freshly caught seafood by the coast, every meal offers an opportunity to experience another side of Sri Lanka.

Rice & Curry

Rice and curry is at the heart of Sri Lankan cuisine. Fragrant rice is accompanied by a selection of vegetable curries, meat or fish dishes, dhal, sambols, pickles, and other traditional accompaniments.

Traditional Favourites

Try crispy hoppers, savoury string hoppers, freshly prepared kottu roti, traditional pittu, or warm roti served with curries and spicy sambols.

Seafood

Enjoy freshly prepared crab, prawns, lobster, squid, tuna, and a variety of local fish. From fiery seafood curries to grilled catches served beside the beach, Sri Lanka offers unforgettable dining experiences for seafood lovers.

Desserts & Sweet Treats

Discover traditional favourites such as curd and treacle, fresh tropical fruits, traditional puddings, ice cream, and other local desserts.

Tropical Fruits

Taste sweet pineapples, bananas, papayas, mangoes, watermelons, and the famous strawberries of the hill country. For something more adventurous, try mangosteen, rambutan, wood apple, passion fruit, or durian.
