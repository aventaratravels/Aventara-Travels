Anuradhapura

Ancient ruins, sacred stupas and a glorious past.

Discover Anuradhapura

Anuradhapura was the first ancient capital of Sri Lanka, ruling for over a millennium. Today, it stands as a sprawling UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the most important archaeological and religious cities in South Asia. The city's massive brick stupas, ancient reservoirs, and intricately carved moonstones are a testament to a highly advanced civilization.

At the heart of the city lies the Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi, a sacred fig tree grown from a cutting of the original tree under which Lord Buddha attained enlightenment. Pilgrims and travellers alike are awestruck by the towering Ruwanwelisaya and Jetavanaramaya stupas. Exploring Anuradhapura by bicycle is a profound journey through Sri Lanka's glorious past.
