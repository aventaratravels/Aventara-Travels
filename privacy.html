Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your personal information.

Last Updated: 11 August 2026

Aventara Travels respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when we may share it, and the rights you may have regarding your personal information when you use our website, contact us, make an enquiry, or arrange travel services through Aventara Travels.

1. What Personal Information Do We Collect?

Depending on how you interact with us and the services you request, we may collect different types of personal information, including Full name, Contact number, Email address, Country of residence & Nationality, Travel dates, Number and ages of travellers, Preferred destinations & Accommodation preferences, Budget, Special requests & Dietary requirements.

2. How We Use Your Personal Information

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Prepare travel quotations & custom itineraries
  • Process and manage bookings
  • Arrange accommodation, transport, and excursions
