Privacy Policy

How Aventara Travels protects, handles, and respects your personal information.

Last Updated: August 2026


Aventara Travels Sri Lanka ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, stored, and protected when you visit https://aventaratravels.com/ or request tour services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details required to design, quote, and execute private travel itineraries in Sri Lanka, including:

  • Full Name, Email Address, and Phone/WhatsApp Number
  • Country of Residence & Arrival Dates
  • Traveler counts, accommodation preferences, and dietary requirements
  • Special requests for safari jeeps, guide chauffeurs, or train tickets

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide customized tour itineraries and price quotations
  • Reserve hotels, transport, national park permits, and safari jeeps
  • Communicate with you regarding trip arrangements via Email or WhatsApp
  • Comply with legal obligations in Sri Lanka

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Personal data is shared only with essential service providers (such as hotel partners and guide chauffeurs) strictly for executing your booked travel itinerary.
