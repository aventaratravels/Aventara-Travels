Romantic Escape

11 Days / 10 Nights

Begin your new chapter together with a romantic journey through the beauty of Sri Lanka. Discover ancient kingdoms, misty mountains, emerald tea plantations, tropical beaches, wildlife-filled national parks, and unforgettable moments created just for two. From the moment you arrive, Aventara Travels takes care of the journey so you can focus on each other.

Day 01 - Airport | Negombo +

Welcome to Sri Lanka

Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport, where your Aventara representative will welcome you and assist you with the beginning of your journey. Transfer to Negombo, a charming coastal destination close to the airport. Known for its golden beach, fishing traditions, lagoon, and colonial heritage, Negombo is the perfect place to unwind after your flight.

Spend your evening relaxing at your hotel and enjoying a romantic dinner together.

Overnight: Negombo

Day 02 - Negombo | Pinnawala | Dambulla | Kandalama +

Elephants & Ancient Heritage

After breakfast, begin your journey towards Sri Lanka's cultural heartland. En route, visit Pinnawala and experience the sight of Sri Lanka's magnificent elephants. Continue towards Dambulla and explore the remarkable Dambulla Cave Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring ancient Buddhist murals, sculptures, and rock-cut shrines.

Continue to Kandalama and check in to your hotel. Enjoy a relaxing evening surrounded by the natural beauty of the region.

Overnight: Kandalama

Day 03 - Kandalama | Sigiriya | Minneriya | Kandalama +

Ancient Fortress & Wildlife Adventure

After breakfast, discover one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarksâ€”Sigiriya Rock Fortress. Explore the ancient gardens, dramatic rock formations, historic remains, and famous frescoes before reaching viewpoints overlooking the surrounding landscape.

In the afternoon, venture into the wilderness on a Minneriya National Park safari. Search for wild elephants and other wildlife while travelling through the park's beautiful dry-zone landscape.

Overnight: Kandalama

Day 04 - Kandalama | Trincomalee +

Journey to the Eastern Coast

After breakfast, leave the cultural triangle behind and travel towards the beautiful east coast. Arrive in Trincomalee, a destination celebrated for its natural harbour, beautiful beaches, turquoise waters, and fascinating cultural heritage.

Spend the remainder of the day relaxing and enjoying the tropical coastal atmosphere.

Overnight: Trincomalee

Day 05 - Trincomalee +

A Day Beside the Ocean

Enjoy a leisurely day together in Trincomalee. Relax at the beach or choose an optional excursion to make your day more adventurous (Pigeon Island Snorkelling, Dolphin Watching, Whale Watching).

Return to your hotel and enjoy a peaceful evening by the coast.

Overnight: Trincomalee

Day 06 - Trincomalee | Kandy +

Culture & the Sacred City

After breakfast, journey into the central highlands towards Kandy, one of Sri Lanka's most culturally significant destinations.

Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, an important spiritual and historical landmark. Continue with a relaxing walk around Kandy Lake and explore the city's local markets. In the evening, experience the vibrant colours, music, and traditional performances of a Sri Lankan cultural show.

Overnight: Kandy

Day 07 - Kandy | Peradeniya | Nuwara Eliya +

Tea Country & Mountain Romance

After breakfast, visit the magnificent Royal Botanic Gardens in Peradeniya. Explore its tropical gardens, colourful flowers, towering trees, and fascinating plant collections.

Continue towards the cool mountain landscape of Nuwara Eliya. Visit a tea plantation and factory to discover how world-famous Ceylon Tea is cultivated and produced. Arrive in Nuwara Eliya and enjoy the cool climate, rolling hills, and romantic atmosphere of Sri Lanka's tea country.

Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

Day 08 - Nuwara Eliya | Kitulgala | Ahungalla +

Waterfalls, Rainforest & Adventure

After breakfast, begin your journey towards Sri Lanka's southern coast. Along the way, stop to admire the spectacular Devon Falls and St. Clair's Falls.

Continue to Kitulgala, a beautiful rainforest destination famous for adventure activities. For couples seeking a little excitement, choose an optional white-water rafting experience on the Kelani River. Continue towards Ahungalla and settle into your coastal hotel.

Overnight: Ahungalla

Day 09 - Ahungalla +

A Relaxing Day for Two

Enjoy a leisurely day at your coastal resort. Take time to relax by the pool, enjoy the beach, or add one of the optional experiences to your itinerary (Madhu River boat safari, Snorkelling, Water sports, etc).

End the day with a beautiful sunset along Sri Lanka's southern coastline.

Overnight: Ahungalla

Day 10 - Ahungalla | Bentota | Colombo +

Coastline to the Capital

After breakfast, travel towards Colombo, Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital. Along the way, enjoy the changing scenery as the tropical coastline gives way to the energy of the city.

Explore Colombo's combination of modern architecture, colonial heritage, cultural landmarks, shopping areas, local markets, restaurants, and entertainment.

Overnight: Colombo

Day 11 - Colombo | Airport +

Until We Meet Again

Enjoy your final breakfast in Sri Lanka. Depending on your flight schedule, spend some free time relaxing, shopping, or enjoying Colombo before your private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport.

Your romantic Sri Lankan escape comes to an end, taking with you unforgettable memories of the island and the time you shared together. Aventara Travels wishes you a safe journey home.
