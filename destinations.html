EXPLORE THE WONDERS OF SRI LANKA

Iconic Destinations

From UNESCO ancient rock fortresses to misty tea estates and golden tropical beaches. Discover the distinct character, cultural value, and charm of Sri Lanka's top tourist destinations.

UNESCO World Heritage • Central Province

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Cultural Value & Importance: Often referred to as the 8th Wonder of the World, Sigiriya is a massive 200-meter-high granite rock fortress built by King Kasyapa in the 5th century AD. It features ancient frescoes of heavenly maidens, the iconic Lion Gate paws, water gardens, and panoramic views over emerald jungle canopy.

Misty Highlands • Badulla District

Ella & Nine Arch Bridge

Place Value & Experience: Nestled in mountain tea country, Ella is world-famous for its dramatic hiking trails (Little Adam's Peak & Ella Rock), waterfalls, and the colonial-era Nine Arch Bridge where trains curve through lush forest ravines. It is the ultimate mountain retreat for couples, hikers, and nature lovers.

EXPLORE ELLA →
Wildlife Sanctuary • Southern & Uva

Yala National Park

Wildlife Value & Safari Quality: Yala boasts one of the highest leopard densities in the world. Visitors experience 4x4 open-top jeep safaris tracking Sri Lankan leopards, wild Asian elephant herds, sloth bears, crocodiles, and over 215 species of coastal and forest birds.

EXPLORE YALA →
UNESCO Heritage • Southern Coast

Galle Fort & Coast

Colonial Heritage Value: Built by the Portuguese in 1588 and fortified by the Dutch, Galle Fort is a living UNESCO fortress. Its cobbled stone ramparts, oceanfront light house, boutique jewelers, art galleries, and gelato cafes overlook the turquoise Indian Ocean.

EXPLORE GALLE →
Sacred Kingdom • Central Highlands

Kandy Sacred City

Sacred Buddhist Capital: The last royal capital of Sri Lankan kings, Kandy houses the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa). Surrounded by misty hills, tea gardens, and Kandy Lake, it is the spiritual heart of Sri Lanka.

EXPLORE KANDY →
Highland Tea Country • Little England

Nuwara Eliya

Tea Culture & Climate: Situated 1,868 meters above sea level, Nuwara Eliya offers crisp mountain air, colonial Tudor bungalows, Victoria Park, Gregory Lake, and world-renowned Ceylon tea plantation tours.
