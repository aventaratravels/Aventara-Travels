Jaffna

Northern heritage, vibrant culture and historic fortresses.

Discover Jaffna

Situated at the northernmost tip of Sri Lanka, Jaffna offers a completely distinct cultural experience from the rest of the island. Rich in Tamil heritage, history, and unique culinary flavours, it is a destination that captivates travellers seeking an authentic, off-the-beaten-path adventure.

Explore the imposing 17th-century Jaffna Fort, originally built by the Portuguese and later expanded by the Dutch. Wander through the vibrant streets, visit the magnificent Nallur Kandaswamy Temple with its towering gopurams, and take a ferry ride to the remote islands of Delft and Nainativu. Jaffna's resilient spirit, welcoming people, and unique coastal landscape make it an unforgettable destination.
