Legal Requirements

Marriage regulations and documentation requirements in Sri Lanka may vary depending on the couple's nationality, marital status, selected marriage registrar, and wedding arrangements. Before confirming your wedding, Aventara Travels can assist with coordinating the required documentation.

Important: Legal requirements, minimum age requirements, residency requirements, documentation deadlines, and ceremony regulations can change. Couples should confirm the current requirements with the relevant Sri Lankan authorities, marriage registrar, and selected wedding venue before making final arrangements.
