Beaches & Sunsets

Golden Sands and Tropical Romance

With over a thousand miles of palm-fringed coastline, Sri Lanka is a tropical paradise for beach lovers. Relax by the warm Indian Ocean, enjoy fresh seafood, and watch the sun dip below the horizon.

Places to Experience

Bentota Beach

A premier resort town on the west coast, known for its long, broad stretches of golden sand. It is ideal for luxury relaxation, romantic walks, and thrilling water sports.

Arugam Bay

Located on the dry east coast, this crescent-shaped bay is world-famous for its surfing breaks. It retains a laid-back, bohemian charm with excellent seafood and beachfront cafes.

Passikudah

Famous for its incredibly shallow and calm turquoise waters. You can wade out into the sea for hundreds of meters, making it one of the safest and most beautiful beaches for swimming.

Hiriketiya Beach

A stunning horseshoe-shaped bay on the south coast that has become a paradise for surfers and digital nomads. It features clear waters, laid-back cafes, and a vibrant surf culture.

Tangalle

Known for its expansive, untouched sandy beaches and rocky coves. Tangalle offers a tranquil escape, perfect for long walks along the shore and watching turtles nest at night.

Dickwella

Home to long stretches of golden sand and a uniquely protective sandbank that makes the waters calm and perfect for swimming year-round, along with the famous Hummanaya Blowhole nearby.
