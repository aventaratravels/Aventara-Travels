Bandarawela

Cool climate, serene landscapes and scenic tranquility.

Discover Bandarawela

Nestled in the lush central highlands of Sri Lanka, Bandarawela is a major tea-producing town known for its exceptionally mild and healthy climate. Surrounded by picturesque tea plantations, pine forests, and misty mountains, it serves as a peaceful retreat away from the bustling coastal cities.

During the colonial era, Bandarawela was a highly sought-after holiday destination for British planters, and traces of this colonial past can still be seen in the town's architecture. Visitors can enjoy refreshing nature walks, explore nearby waterfalls, and experience the slow, serene pace of life in the Sri Lankan hill country. It is also an excellent base for exploring the famous attractions of Ella and Haputale.
