Itinerary Overview

Day 01 - Airport | Negombo +

Airport | Negombo

Meet and assist on arrival at Airport by Aventara Travels Representative. Transfer from Airport to Negombo. Just seven kilometres from the Bandaranaike International Airport, at the tip of the Negombo lagoon, lays the pristine Negombo beach. Negombo is a famous fishing village that is proud of its colonial Dutch fort and other Dutch period architecture.

Savour a delectable dinner at a hotel in Negombo before retiring for the night to rest and recover from your flight.

Day 02 - Ayurveda & Wellness Retreat +

Ayurveda & Wellness Retreat

Treat yourself to a blissful Ayurveda retreat that incorporates a myriad of authentic treatments and traditional techniques, rooted in an ancient healing form unique to the island. Escape from the stresses of modern living.

Experience the healing power of yoga, rejuvenate your mind and soul with guided meditation, and indulge in therapeutic spa treatments utilizing special mixes of essential oils. Find a sense of inner peace amidst mother nature while embarking on a comprehensive wellness journey towards true rejuvenation.

Day 06 - Colombo City Tour +

Colombo City Tour

Proceed to the city of Colombo, the vibrant commercial capital of Sri Lanka. Witness the amazing beauty of this place that fosters a richly diverse population and a seamless blend of the old and the new.

Take a tour of the city and visit iconic landmarks including the Old Parliament, Independence Memorial Hall, National Museum, and the beautiful Gangaramaya Temple. Spend some time shopping at local boutiques or the Pettah Bazaar before enjoying a farewell dinner at a local restaurant renowned for its authentic Sri Lankan cuisine.

Day 07 - Departure +

Departure

Enjoy a hearty breakfast at your hotel and spend the morning at your leisure, perhaps picking up some last-minute souvenirs or taking one final stroll along the beach.

Proceed to the Bandaranaike International Airport in time for your departure flight. Aventara Travels wishes you a safe flight back home, carrying unforgettable memories of your tropical island getaway.
