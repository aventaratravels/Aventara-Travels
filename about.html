OUR ORIGINS & STORY

Rooted in Sri Lankan Hospitality

Aventara Travels Sri Lanka is a licensed travel agency and Destination Management Company (DMC) based on Nuwaraeliya Road, Keppetipola, Sri Lanka.

Founded with a passion for authentic island travel, we connect international visitors with Sri Lanka’s UNESCO world heritage sites, misty highland tea estates, wild elephant national parks, and palm-fringed tropical coastlines through seamless private chauffeur-guided tours.

INTERNATIONAL DMC REACH

Serving Global Travelers

We serve travelers and B2B travel partners across key international markets including Germany, Australia, the Netherlands, China, India, the UK, USA, France, Italy, and Switzerland.

With a dedicated fleet of modern air-conditioned luxury sedans, SUVs, and vans—driven by friendly, professional English-speaking guide chauffeurs—we ensure 24/7 ground handling excellence.
