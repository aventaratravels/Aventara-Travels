Sri Lanka Honeymoon Tours

10 Days / 09 Nights

Plan the most romantic island escape with our Sri Lanka honeymoon tour! Indulge in a romantic getaway unlike any other and make treasured memories which will last a life time. Our package offers you the perfect chance to immerse yourself in a magical island experience while offering you the chance to experience luxurious stays at premier resorts, visit top attractions and more.

Day 01 - Pinnawala | Kandalama +

Arrival & Elephant Orphanage

Greet, meet and assistance at the Colombo International Airport and proceed to Pinnawala.

The Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage provides visitors with the best chance of seeing a large number of elephants at close quarters. The highlight of the visit will undoubtably be adorable baby elephants being bottled fed and the daily bath sessions the herd enjoys twice a day.

Proceed to Kandalama and leisure in the evening... Enjoy a unique and a special cave dinner in Kandalama. By the light of flaming torches and candle lanterns, serenaded by a lone flautist, this dinner is sure to bring out the romance in your soul.

Overnight: Kandalama

Day 02 - Kandalama | Sigiriya | Kandalama +

Lion Rock Fortress

After breakfast proceed to Sigiriya. Climb the fifth century Sigiriya Rock Fortress, built by King Kashyapa (477-495 AD). The ''Lion Rock'' is a citadel of unusual beauty rising 200 metres from the scrub jungle. Visit the world-renowned frescoes of the ''Cloud Maidens'' of Sigiriya.

Towards the end of the evening, indulge in a couple's spa treatments to help rejuvenate mind, body, and soul. The special Aphrodite mix of essential oils sets the mood, while chocolates, fruits, and a bottle of Champagne completes the experience.

Overnight: Kandalama

Day 03 - Kandalama | Matale | Kandy +

Spice Gardens & Sacred City

Breakfast at hotel. Pay a visit to a spice garden in Matale, to learn more about the various spices that the island of Sri Lanka has long since been famous for.

Proceed to Kandy, a World Heritage Site. Visitors will be able to take in the Sri Dalada Maligawa, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. Take a stroll under shady pathways bordering the Kandy Lake and head towards the market. Thereafter, enjoy a cultural show.

Overnight: Kandy

Day 04 - Kandy | Peradeniya | Nuwara Eliya +

Gardens & Scenic Train Ride

Breakfast at hotel. Visit Peradeniya Botanical Garden. Sprawling across 147 acres, the Peradeniya Botanical Gardens is a walker's paradise.

Proceed to Nuwara Eliya by Train. Feast your eyes on the scenic views from the comfort of your observation carriage. Enjoy a unique dining experience on board as the train winds its way through the mountain valleys. Your car will be waiting for you at Nanu Oya.

Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

Day 05 - Nuwara Eliya +

Little England

Breakfast at the hotel. Visit a sprawling tea estate and factory, where you will be able to learn how the finest tea in the world is made.

Tour Nuwara Eliya. Take a stroll along the banks of Lake Gregory, after which you can take in the emerald links of the Victoria Golf Course. Indulge in some freshly picked strawberries at a local strawberry farm.

Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

Day 06 - Nuwara Eliya | Yala +

Journey to the Wild

Breakfast at the hotel. Transfer from Nuwara Eliya to Yala.

Check-in to a Yala hotel and enjoy the evening at leisure in the wilderness.

Overnight: Yala

Day 07 - Yala | Bentota +

Safari & Coastline

Early morning safari in Yala National Park. Spanning across 1,259 square kilometres, the sanctuary is home to large herds of wild elephants, wild boars, buffaloes, deer, and crocodiles. It's also one of the best places to catch a glimpse of the famous Sri Lankan leopard.

After breakfast proceed to Bentota for your beach stay. Escape to a secluded heaven.

Overnight: Bentota

Day 08 - Bentota | Mirissa | Bentota +

Whale Watching

Start your day early and head to the chic little coastal town of Mirissa. It's among the top hot spots to catch sight of some majestic blue and sperm whales emerging out of the depths just off the coast. You will also be able to watch the frolicking antics of large pods of spinner, bottlenose and striped dolphins!

Rest of the day at leisure.

Overnight: Bentota

Day 09 - Bentota | Galle | Madu River Safari | Colombo +

Heritage & River Safari

Tour Galle: Wander around the UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Dutch Fort. Walk along its cobbled streets lined with carefully preserved colonial buildings.

Boat Ride in Madu River: Enjoy a serene boat ride down the tranquil Madu River. Bask under the warm tropical sun, as you take in the thriving wildlife inhabiting the shady mangroves.

Proceed to Colombo.

Overnight: Colombo

Day 10 - Airport +

Departure

Breakfast at the Hotel.

Transfer from Colombo to Airport for your onward journey, taking with you a lifetime of memories.
