Sri Lanka Tours from India

Aventara Travels creates private and tailor-made Sri Lanka holidays for travellers from India. Whether you are looking for a short escape, family holiday, honeymoon, cultural journey, wildlife adventure or relaxing beach vacation, we design your itinerary around your interests, travel dates and budget.

Sri Lanka is a compact island with an extraordinary variety of experiences. In one journey you can discover ancient cities, Buddhist heritage, tea plantations, mountain landscapes, national parks and tropical beaches.

Popular Destinations

Colombo

Discover Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital, modern city life, restaurants, shopping and heritage.

Kandy

Explore the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, cultural traditions and beautiful mountain scenery.

Sigiriya

Discover the famous Lion Rock and the surrounding cultural and natural attractions.

Dambulla

Explore the famous cave temple complex and ancient Buddhist art.

Nuwara Eliya
