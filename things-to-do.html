Top Things to Do in Sri Lanka

From legendary mountain train journeys to private leopard safaris and surfing tropical waves. Explore the top bucket-list activities in Sri Lanka curated by Aventara Travels.

HIGHLAND EXPERIENCE

1. Scenic Kandy to Ella Train Journey

Hailed as one of the most beautiful train trips in the world, this scenic ride winds through emerald tea plantations, misty mountain passes, pine forests, and high viaducts like the Nine Arch Bridge.

UNESCO HERITAGE

2. Climb Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress

Ascend 1,200 steps to the summit of King Kasyapa’s 5th-century sky palace. Marvel at ancient frescoes, the Mirror Wall, massive stone lion paws, and 360-degree jungle views.

WILDLIFE & SAFARI

3. Track Leopards & Elephants in Yala

Embark on an early morning or afternoon open-top 4x4 jeep safari in Yala or Udawalawe National Park to encounter wild leopards, sloth bears, and Asian elephant herds.

CULTURAL & TASTING

4. Ceylon Tea Plucking & Tasting

Walk through lush tea bushes alongside local tea pluckers, tour an authentic vintage tea factory, learn the grading process, and sample single-origin Ceylon tea.

COASTAL ADVENTURE

5. Whale & Dolphin Watching in Mirissa

Board a boat from Mirissa Harbor to spot Blue Whales—the largest creatures on Earth—along with spinner dolphins, sperm whales, and sea turtles in the warm Indian Ocean.

HERITAGE WALK

6. Sunset Walk on Galle Fort Ramparts

Stroll along the historic 17th-century Dutch ramparts of Galle Fort as the sun dips into the sea. Explore cobbled lanes, boutique shops, and coral stone bastions.
