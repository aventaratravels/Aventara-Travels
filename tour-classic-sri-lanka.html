Classic Sri Lanka

10 Days / 9 Nights - A Timeless Journey

Immerse yourself in the classic beauty of Sri Lanka. This 10-day itinerary takes you on a comprehensive journey through ancient cities, lush highlands, wild savannas, and tranquil beaches, offering a well-paced and deeply enriching travel experience.

Itinerary Outline

DAY 01 - Airport | Colombo +

Arrive in Sri Lanka and transfer to Colombo for an introductory city tour and a relaxing evening.

DAY 02 - Colombo | Anuradhapura +

Travel to the ancient capital of Anuradhapura and explore its magnificent stupas and ruins.

DAY 03 - Anuradhapura | Sigiriya +

Visit the majestic Sigiriya Rock Fortress and discover the nearby Dambulla Cave Temple.

DAY 04 - Sigiriya | Kandy +

Head to Kandy, visiting a spice garden en route. In the evening, witness the revered Temple of the Tooth.

DAY 05 - Kandy | Peradeniya +

Spend a relaxed morning exploring the Royal Botanic Gardens and the scenic Kandy Lake.

DAY 06 - Kandy | Ella +

Take the world-famous scenic train journey through the emerald mountains to the picturesque village of Ella.

DAY 07 - Ella | Yala +

Hike to Little Adam's Peak before descending to the southern plains to prepare for a wildlife adventure.

DAY 08 - Yala National Park +

Embark on an early morning safari in Yala National Park to spot leopards, elephants, and bears.

DAY 09 - Yala | Galle | Bentota +

Travel along the south coast, explore the Dutch Fort of Galle, and settle into a luxury beach resort in Bentota.

DAY 10 - Bentota | Airport +

Relax on the beach before transferring to the airport for your onward journey.
