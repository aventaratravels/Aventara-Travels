Polonnaruwa

Medieval capital, ancient temples and monumental statues.

Discover Polonnaruwa

Following the decline of Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa rose to prominence as Sri Lanka's second great capital in the 11th century. The ruins of this medieval city are exceptionally well-preserved and are clustered closer together, making it an incredibly rewarding archaeological site to explore.

The highlight of Polonnaruwa is the Gal Viharaya, a magnificent rock temple where four colossal images of Lord Buddha have been carved flawlessly into a single granite rock face. Visitors can also marvel at the ruins of the Royal Palace, the highly detailed Vatadage, and the massive Parakrama Samudra (Sea of Parakrama), a man-made reservoir that showcases the incredible engineering skills of the era.
