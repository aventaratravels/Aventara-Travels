7 DAYS / 6 NIGHTS PRIVATE TOUR

Cultural Highlights Tour

Negombo Lagoon • Anuradhapura Ancient City • Polonnaruwa Medieval Capital • Sigiriya Rock Fortress • Kandy Sacred City • Galle Fort

DAY BY DAY ITINERARY DETAILS

Detailed Day-by-Day Experience

DAY 01 Airport | Negombo Coastal Fishing Village
▼

Meet and assist on arrival at Airport by Aventara Travels Representative. Transfer from Airport to Negombo. Just seven kilometres from the Bandaranaike International Airport, at the tip of the Negombo lagoon, lays the pristine Negombo beach. Negombo is a famous fishing village that is proud of its colonial Dutch fort and other Dutch period architecture.

Savour a delectable dinner at a hotel in Negombo before retiring for the night to rest and recover from your flight.

DAY 02 Anuradhapura Ancient City (First Capital & UNESCO Site)
▼

Step back in time as you explore Anuradhapura, the first ancient capital of Sri Lanka and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Traverse the city to discover historic manmade reservoirs, monumental stupas built by great kings, and beautifully preserved ruins of ancient palaces and monasteries.

Pay your respects at the sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi, which originated from a branch of the sacred bo tree in India under which Lord Buddha attained enlightenment. Witness the ethereal charm of this ancient civilization before enjoying a relaxing evening.

DAY 03 Polonnaruwa | Medieval Capital & Gal Viharaya
▼

Journey to Polonnaruwa, the second most ancient of Sri Lanka's kingdoms. This UNESCO World Heritage site remains one of the best planned archaeological relic sites in the country, standing testimony to the discipline and greatness of the Kingdom's first rulers.

Explore the well-preserved ruins by bicycle or vehicle, witnessing magnificent statues, temples, and palaces, including the famous Gal Viharaya, which features three massive Buddha figures carved out of a single granite rock face. Return to your hotel and enjoy a serene evening.

DAY 04 - 07 Sigiriya Lion Rock Citadel • Kandy Sacred City • Galle Fort
▼

Climb the 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress at sunrise, visit spice gardens in Matale, and attend a traditional cultural dance show in Kandy. Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic before departing through Nuwara Eliya tea estates to colonial Galle Fort and Colombo Airport.
